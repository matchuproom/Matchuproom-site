Questions

The fine print

When can I use it?

Waitlist members get first access to a free beta during the fantasy playoffs. The full app launches before 2027 draft season.

How much will it cost?

There will be a free tier. Pro will cost less than most fantasy subscriptions, and founding members from this waitlist get locked-in launch pricing.

Which leagues does it work with?

Any league on any platform. Add your roster once by searching your players, set your lineup spots (Superflex, kickers and defenses included), and choose PPR, half-PPR or standard.

Where does the data come from?

Play-by-play, charting, coverage, depth chart, injury and betting-line data from open NFL data sources, refreshed daily and turned into one matchup grade with the reasons shown.
