The fine print

When can I use it?

Waitlist members get first access to a free beta during the fantasy playoffs. The full app launches before 2027 draft season.

How much will it cost?

There will be a free tier. Pro will cost less than most fantasy subscriptions, and founding members from this waitlist get locked-in launch pricing.

Which leagues does it work with?

Any league. You can look up any player. League sync starts with Sleeper, then ESPN and Yahoo.

Where does the data come from?

Play-by-play, charting and usage data from licensed and open NFL data sources, turned into one matchup score with the reasons shown.
