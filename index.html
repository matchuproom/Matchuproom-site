Questions

The fine print

Can I use it right now?

Yes. Open app.matchuproom.com on your phone or computer. The free version needs no account; sign in with Google or your email to save your team and go Premium.

How much does it cost?

Free forever for the weekly board, top-12 rankings and 3 start/sit checks a week. The Full Room is $14.99 for the rest of the season (one payment, through the Super Bowl) or $4.99 a month. Code GABE takes 20% off the season pass.

Which leagues does it work with?

Connect a Sleeper or ESPN league in about a minute (private ESPN leagues too) and your rosters, matchups and free agents come in automatically. Any other platform works by adding your players yourself. PPR, half-PPR and standard, Superflex, kickers and defenses included.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Monthly plans cancel anytime from your account, and you keep Premium until the month ends. Any first purchase can be fully refunded within 7 days: email gabe@matchuproom.com.

Is there an app?

Yes: Matchup Room is on the App Store for iPhone (Android is next). The iPhone app is in final review with Apple and lands on the App Store very soon (Android is next). Until then, it runs in any phone's browser: open app.matchuproom.com, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Where does the data come from?

Play-by-play, charting, depth chart, injury and betting-line data from open NFL data sources (nflverse and FTN charting), refreshed every few hours and turned into one matchup grade with the reasons shown.
